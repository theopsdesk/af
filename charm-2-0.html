My work on Charm 2.0

I owned the launch side of Charm 2.0, working directly with the CEO. Marketing, the website, the customer systems and the reporting were built together, so a visitor's path from the first ad to a reserved pendant could be seen from end to end.

Website

The Shopify site, the reservation page and the quiz that qualifies visitors, built phone first and managed every day.

Checkout

The deposit flow on Stripe, connected to a thank you page and to tracking.

Tracking

Meta Pixel with custom events for quiz steps, scroll, time on page, reserve clicks and drop off, set in Shopify's customer events so a theme edit cannot break it.

Campaigns

Meta lead campaigns across ad sets and creative angles, including retargeting for visitors who left no email.

Email and leads

Quiz leads sent to Klaviyo and a sheet by webhook, with email opens and clicks reported back.

Dashboard

An n8n dashboard combining Shopify, Meta Ads, Klaviyo and Stripe into one page for the CEO, covering sales, ad spend, cost per lead, ROAS and profit.

Creative

Weekly marketing assets planned and reviewed before launch.

Community

Charm Circle, the membership community, set up with free and paid access.

App

The animated emotion state wheel for the mobile app, built in Flutter and Dart.

Tools Shopify, Liquid, Klaviyo, Stripe, Meta Pixel, Meta Ads Manager, n8n, Cloudflare Workers, Google Apps Script, Flutter, Dart, Figma.
