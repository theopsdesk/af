Stay Safe
Scam Warning

Recruitment fraud is a documented and serious problem in Indonesia. The Indonesian Ministry of Communications has issued official warnings. Know these rules before applying anywhere.

Rule 1 · The Most Important RuleAny job that asks you to pay money at any stage is a scam. Registration fees, training fees, uniform fees, ID verification fees, anything. Real employers do not charge candidates. Walk away immediately and block the number.
Red Flag 1
They ask for money at any stage

Registration fee, training fee, uniform deposit, ID card fee. Any payment request from a recruiter is a scam, no exceptions.

Red Flag 2
The offer is too good and too fast

You applied two hours ago and already have a salary offer without an interview. Real hiring takes time. Suspiciously fast offers are always fraudulent.

Red Flag 3
Telegram-only contact with no company website

Legitimate companies have verifiable websites, LinkedIn pages, and official email addresses. No verifiable company presence means do not proceed.

Red Flag 4
They ask for your bank account upfront

No legitimate employer needs bank details before you have signed a contract and started work. Sharing this early can lead to identity theft.
